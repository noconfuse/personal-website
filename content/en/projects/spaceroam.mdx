Model's done — then what? SpaceRoam is my answer: a platform that turns 3D models into immersive roaming experiences.

Upload a GLB / GLTF model, set the environment, lighting and starting camera in the editor, attach interactions to nodes or points, and publish to get a standalone link. Visitors need no login — they open the link and walk through the model in first person.

## How it works

Three steps: Edit → Preview → Publish.

**Edit**: Upload a single file or a zip (up to 200MB). Configure environment maps, lighting, starting views and color grading, with automatic ground setup and free-fly mode built in. Two kinds of interaction anchors — nodes and points — support popups, links, Q&A and audio (with distance falloff).

**Preview**: Walk through it yourself first to check the experience.

**Publish**: One click generates a standalone link. On desktop, click to move; on mobile, dual joysticks — landscape recommended.

## Why I built it

The traditional way of delivering 3D models is bad: zips that won't open, software the client doesn't have, screenshots that can't explain spatial relationships. SpaceRoam turns "delivering files" into "delivering experiences" — one link, first-person roaming, interactions viewable anytime. It dramatically improves communication.

## Use cases

- **City / campus showcases**: digital twin models as roaming links, no special software needed.
- **Factory / equipment walkthroughs**: attach audio/video guides to parts for training, sales and remote acceptance.
- **Exhibitions / digitization**: museums and exhibition halls as self-guided tours with automatic narration.
- **Education / training**: chemical plants, surgical procedures — repeatable roaming spaces.

It's not a game — no physics engine, no complex interactions. GLB / GLTF only for now; convert other formats first.
