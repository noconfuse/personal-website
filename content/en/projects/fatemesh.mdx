## What it is

**FateMesh** ([deerblock.top](https://deerblock.top)) is an AI divination agent platform I designed and shipped solo. It blends Eastern divination with generative AI, letting practitioners create their own AI "Lingxi" in 5 minutes without writing a line of code — an alter ego that actually performs readings and remembers every client.

The platform covers Bazi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Tarot, Astrology and Da Liu Ren, works in both Chinese and English, and serves both creators and end users.

## The problem

Traditional divination going online has two pain points: a practitioner's time doesn't scale past one-on-one sessions, and the "AI fortune telling" products out there are just chat wrappers — they can't cast real charts and they don't remember clients. FateMesh's answer: build the actual divination tools (chart casting, card drawing) into the agent's capability layer, so the AI can "cast, interpret, and remember" like a real practitioner — then make creators' expertise productized and billable through a marketplace.

## Core features

**1. Create a Lingxi in 5 minutes (no-code agent building)**
- Pick a specialty: Tarot, Bazi, Zi Wei, Astrology, Da Liu Ren, counseling, or a custom niche
- Upload private knowledge-base docs, or use built-in schools — your Lingxi studies them like you would
- Share a link or list on the marketplace; it serves clients 24/7

**2. An agent that casts, not just chats**
- Real divination tools built in: Tarot draws, Bazi charts, Zi Wei charts, Da Liu Ren calculations
- Knowledge base + long-term memory: your Lingxi remembers every client's history and preferences
- MCP protocol support to connect external tools — act, not just answer

![FateMesh chat preview](/projects/fatemesh1.jpg)

**3. Creator verification & profile pages**
- Apply online, get verified, become an official creator
- Dedicated profile: avatar, bio, specialties, service languages

![FateMesh creator profile](/projects/fatemesh2.jpg)

**4. Content studio**
- One-click Tarot/chart analysis cards, visual assets, and post copy
- Built-in templates: daily draws, horoscopes, social posts, short-video scripts, livestream openers

![FateMesh content studio](/projects/fatemesh3.jpg)

**5. Marketplace & credits**
- Lingxi agents can be listed into the marketplace and featured sections
- Transparent billing: either the creator or the user pays — no hidden fees
- Monthly settlement by conversation volume, with credit rewards for creators
- Trending rankings use a composite scoring algorithm, recalculated every 6 hours (30-day conversations 35%, favorites 25%, creator followers 20%, new listings 10%, rating 10%)

## Technical notes

- **Front-end**: React + Vite SPA, dark theme, full zh/en internationalization
- **Agent architecture**: built-in divination tools + knowledge-base retrieval (RAG) + long-term memory, extended via MCP
- **SEO**: complete Meta / Open Graph / Twitter Card / hreflang annotations
- **Engineering**: ~400KB Vite bundle (pre-gzip), merged first-screen assets, controlled performance
